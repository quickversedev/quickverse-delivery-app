QuickVerse Rider

If you wish to delete your QuickVerse Rider account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to seenasingh81@gmail.com from the email or phone number associated with your account.
  2. Include the subject line: "Account Deletion Request — QuickVerse Rider"
  3. In the email body, provide your registered phone number so we can locate your account.

What Gets Deleted

Upon processing your request, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your account profile and phone number
  • Location history
  • Device registration and push notification tokens
  • Any uploaded payment proof images

What May Be Retained

Certain data may be retained for a limited period as required by law or for legitimate business purposes:

  • Completed delivery records and earnings history (retained for up to 180 days for accounting and tax compliance)
  • Transaction records as required by applicable financial regulations

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days of receipt. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted.
